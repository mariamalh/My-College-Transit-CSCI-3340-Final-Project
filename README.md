# My College Transit (MCT)

My College Transit is a web application that helps bridge the gap between on-campus transportation (buses and shuttles) and off-campus transportation (buses) for students and faculty at UTRGV. It shows nearby transit information, such as the location, arrival/departure times, and routes of bus/shuttle stops and how connections can be made to most optimally reach a desired location.
